## Voice and tone

marketch.uz speaks like a market clerk: short, concrete, and calm. Prices are estimates and intentions, not guaranteed trades.

### Principles

- Name the crop, place, amount, and unit.
- Say what the user can do next.
- Call offers “intentions,” not orders or trades.

### Examples

- Weak: “An error occurred.”
- Strong: “We couldn’t publish this intention. Check the product, region, and price, then try again.”

- Weak: “Submit”
- Strong: “Publish intention”

- Weak: “No data.”
- Strong: “No intentions match these filters. Reset the filters, or publish a new intention.”

### Tone

- Routine actions: neutral and brief.
- Errors: say what failed and how to recover. Do not blame the user.
- Forecasts: state that the figure is an estimate.
