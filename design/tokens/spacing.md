## Spacing tokens

- `spacing-1`: 4px
- `spacing-2`: 8px
- `spacing-3`: 12px
- `spacing-4`: 16px
- `spacing-5`: 24px
- `spacing-6`: 32px
- `spacing-7`: 40px
- `spacing-8`: 56px

### Radii

- `radius-sm`: 6px — buttons, inputs, chips.
- `radius-md`: 10px — cards.
- `radius-lg`: 16px — dialogs.
- `radius-pill`: 999px — search and timeframe.

### Usage

- Inside a control (label to field, icon to text): `spacing-1` to `spacing-3`.
- Between fields and related controls: `spacing-3` or `spacing-4`.
- Between page sections: `spacing-5`.
- Page gutters: `spacing-4` on narrow screens, `spacing-5` on wide screens.
