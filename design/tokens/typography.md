## Typography tokens

- **UI font**: Inter, then system-ui.
- **Numeric font**: Inter with tabular figures, for prices, volumes, and times.
- **Base size**: 16px.
- **Line height**: 1.5 for body, 1.2 for titles.

### Roles

- `heading-xl`: 32px, Inter, weight 600. One product title per view.
- `heading-m`: 20px, Inter, weight 600. Section titles (Bozor, Intentions).
- `heading-s`: 16px, Inter, weight 600. Side panels.
- `body`: 16px, regular.
- `body-small`: 14px, regular. Tables, controls, and secondary copy.
- `caption`: 13px, regular. Metadata and field labels.

Do not add other font sizes.
